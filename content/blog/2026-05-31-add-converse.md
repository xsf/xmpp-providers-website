Another [XMPP app](https://xmpp.org/software/?category=clients) has added XMPP Providers support to its onboarding.
[Converse.js](https://conversejs.org/), an XMPP web client, allows to select a choice of XMPP providers during account registration.
The web app is hosted by many XMPP operators to provide their users a decent web experience.
We are very happy about this development and further distrubtion.
Using XMPP Providers will enhance the onboarding experience for many people across the entire XMPP universe.

## More Automation

To continue enabling true federation and reduce manual work, the creation of merge requests to add new providers has been automated.
One simply needs to follow the steps to [add a missing provider](/add-provider/).
Once an issue has been created, our bot will create a merge request including an inital check to update the provider's properties.
This will allow us to not just focus on ensuring the quality but also process more of the growing demand of operators to be listed.

Thanks to everyone for being patient with us but also running their service.
Moreover, many thanks to the [XMPP apps](/apps/) for supporting this service as well as using it for their onboarding.
That is the best way of using the XMPP Provider service!

### Improve the XMPP Onboarding Experience

For a good user experience, [apps integrating XMPP Providers](/apps/) are as important as the [providers](/overview/) themselves.
If you are an XMPP developer, please consider [adding XMPP Providers support](https://invent.kde.org/melvo/xmpp-providers#usage) to your app.
If you are an operator of a public XMPP service, [add your service to the list](/add-provider/).

You can also consider to run your own XMPP server and become a public provider.
With our [automated XMPP server setup](https://invent.kde.org/melvo/xmpp-providers-server), you can operate a fully-featured XMPP server with minimal effort.

Feel free to [reach out to us](/contact/) if you have any questions!
If you like to support XMPP Providers, please consider [making a donation](https://liberapay.com/xmpp_providers).
[Follow us and spread the word](https://fosstodon.org/@xmpp_providers)!
